## Verse of Infalibility of Ahlul Bayt (pbut)
### The verse of Tat-heer (Purification) [33:33](https://quran.com/33/33)
It is widely known that the Qur'an as recorded in writing is not in the order of its revelation. However, a common misunderstanding is that any single numbered verse would have been revealed at the same time as a single unit. That has not always been the case.

Before looking at 33:33 we can examine the famous verse of 5:3 to see an example of this phenomenon.

- You are prohibited carrion, blood, the flesh of swine, and what has been offered to other than Allah, and the animal strangled or beaten to death, and that which dies by falling or is gored to death, and that which is mangled by a beast of prey—barring that which you may purify —and what is sacrificed on stone altars \[to idols\], and that you should divide by raffling with arrows. All that is transgression. Today the faithless have despaired of your religion. So do not fear them, but fear Me. **Today I have perfected your religion for you, and I have completed My blessing upon you, and I have approved Islam as your religion.** But should anyone be compelled by hunger, without inclining to sin, then Allah is indeed all-forgiving, all-merciful.

The first and last parts of this verse, discussing food law, were revealed a year or so earlier to the highlighted portion that was revealed in 10 AH. It is commonly called the _Ikmal_ verse, which, as you can see, is actually a part of the Qur'anic verse 5:3 rather than the entirety of it.

So in a similar manner 33:33 is also made up of parts that were not all revealed together.

- Stay in your houses and do not flaunt your finery like the former \[days of pagan\] ignorance. Maintain the prayer and pay the zakat, and obey Allah and His Apostle. **Indeed Allah desires to repel all impurity from you, O People of the Household, and purify you with a thorough purification.**

The Qur'anic commentaries from all schools of thought in Islam demonstrate that the second part, famously called _Ayat al-Tathir_, was revealed separately and distinctly from the previous portion of 33:33 and also the subsequent verses.

Just like the mention of food law in 5:3 shows the continuity of the food law theme before and after the completion of religion theme, so also here the use of the all-feminine gender in the grammar of the part of 33:33 before the _Ayat al-Tathir_ and afterwards in 33:34 onwards shows that those sentences were on the theme addressing the wives of the Prophet (s).

The _Ayat al-Tathir_ part, on the other hand, uses the masculine gender that is grammatically appropriate when addressing a set of people that contains at least one male.

The reference to the Ahl al-Bayt in 33:33, therefore, is not a continuation of the address towards the wives of the Prophet (s), but an independent theme addressing 'Ali, Fatima, Hasan, and Husayn, on them all be peace, as the chosen Family of the Prophet Muhammad (s).

### The verse of Mawaddah [42:23](https://quran.com/42/23)
This verse emphasizes the importance of loving and respecting the Ahlul Bayt (pbut). It indicates that Allah has commanded the believers to show love and affection towards them. This love is not just a mere emotional attachment but also a recognition of their high status and role as guides for the Muslim community.

### The verse of Mubahala [3:61](https://quran.com/3/61)
This verse refers to the event of Mubahala, where the Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) was commanded by Allah to invite the Christians of Najran to a debate. The Prophet brought along his closest family members, including Ali, Fatimah, Hasan, and Husayn (pbut). This indicates that they were chosen by Allah to represent the Prophet and his message. The verse emphasizes their high status and the importance of their role in Islam.

### The verse of Wilayah [5:55](https://quran.com/5/55)
This verse refers to the appointment of Ali (p) as the leader and guardian of the Muslim community. It emphasizes that Allah has chosen him as a protector and guide for the believers. This indicates that Ali (p) and the Ahlul Bayt (pbut) have a special status in Islam and are infallible in their guidance.

### The verse of Taqwa [2:2](https://quran.com/2/2)
This verse emphasizes the importance of Taqwa (piety) in Islam. It indicates that those who are truly pious and righteous are the ones who are guided by Allah. The Ahlul Bayt (pbut) are known for their high level of Taqwa and righteousness, indicating that they are infallible in their actions and guidance.

### The verse of Imamate [2:124](https://quran.com/2/124)
This verse refers to the appointment of Ibrahim (p) as a leader and guide for the people. It indicates that Allah has chosen certain individuals to be leaders and guides for the Muslim community. The Ahlul Bayt (pbut) are considered to be the rightful successors of Ibrahim (p) and are therefore infallible in their guidance.

### The verse of Shura [42:38](https://quran.com/42/38)
This verse emphasizes the importance of consultation and mutual agreement in decision-making. It indicates that those who are truly righteous and guided by Allah will consult with one another and make decisions based on mutual agreement. The Ahlul Bayt (pbut) are known for their high level of wisdom and guidance, indicating that they are infallible in their decision-making.

### The verse of Khums [8:41](https://quran.com/8/41)
This verse refers to the obligation of Khums (a form of charity) in Islam. It indicates that a portion of the wealth of the believers should be given to the Prophet and his family. This emphasizes the special status of the Ahlul Bayt (pbut) and their role as guides for the Muslim community. It also indicates that they are infallible in their actions and guidance.

### The verse of Zakat [9:60](https://quran.com/9/60)
This verse refers to the obligation of Zakat (charity) in Islam. It indicates that a portion of the wealth of the believers should be given to those who are in need, including the Prophet and his family. This emphasizes the special status of the Ahlul Bayt (pbut) and their role as guides for the Muslim community. It also indicates that they are infallible in their actions and guidance.

### The verse of Sadaqat [9:103](https://quran.com/9/103)
This verse refers to the obligation of Sadaqat (charity) in Islam. It indicates that a portion of the wealth of the believers should be given to those who are in need, including the Prophet and his family. This emphasizes the special status of the Ahlul Bayt (pbut) and their role as guides for the Muslim community. It also indicates that they are infallible in their actions and guidance.

### The verse of Fadak [28:76](https://quran.com/28/76)
This verse refers to the story of Qarun (Korah) and his wealth. It indicates that wealth and material possessions do not determine a person's worth or status in the eyes of Allah. The Ahlul Bayt (pbut) are known for their humility and piety, indicating that they are infallible in their actions and guidance.
